## Placement

Immediately after:

> General-purpose models know a great deal about the world. They know less about your world.

Caption:

> The model reasons only over the part of reality made visible to it.

## Idea

Make the **context gap** memorable: the distance between what a general model knows and what it needs for *this* problem.

## Metaphor

A small illuminated working area surrounded by a much larger technical system hidden in darkness. The AI has access only to the illuminated fragment; additional context progressively reveals the surrounding architecture.

## Composition

- 1600 × 900, 16:9
- Large negative space
- One dominant illuminated fragment in the foreground or midground
- Surrounding architecture / structure implied in shadow
- No embedded title or explanatory text
- Safe zone: important content inside central 80%

## Style

Editorial technical abstraction:

- dark or neutral environment
- geometric / architectural forms
- subtle depth
- restrained palette
- sophisticated rather than futuristic

## Must not appear

- glowing AI brain
- humanoid robot
- neon / cyberpunk
- stock-photo people
- corporate isometric infographic look
- baked-in typography or labels explaining the metaphor

## Relation to thesis

Instruction alone is a compass without a map. Context is what reveals the rest of the system. The image should make that asymmetry felt, not explained in words.
